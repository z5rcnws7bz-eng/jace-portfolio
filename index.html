Computational Hemodynamics and Physics-Guided Machine Learning for Arterial Stenosis Prediction

↳ Used SimVascular to model blood flow through idealized arteries with 0%, 30%, 50%, and 70% narrowing. On a held-out 50% stenosis geometry, a physics-guided model reduced axial velocity prediction error (RMSE) by 25.7% compared with the analytical baseline, but missed regions of reverse flow. The 70% simulation results were preliminary.

↳ Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) · SimVascular · Physics-Guided Machine Learning · research code

After the Break — Receiver Cut Mechanics and Rookie NFL Separation

↳ For the 2027 NFL Big Data Bowl University Track, used Combine tracking data to measure how 59 wide receivers slowed, turned, and regained speed. In ridge regression tests that held out one draft year at a time, adding those measurements increased prediction error for rookie separation at pass release from 0.257 to 0.294 yards (RMSE). The measurements helped frame questions for film review.

↳ Player Tracking · Python · Ridge Regression · Draft-Year Validation · public Kaggle notebook
