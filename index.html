Reliability of Molecular Toxicity Prediction on ClinTox Across Curation, Chemical Splits, and Training Repeats

Independent research manuscript

↳ Examined how reliably molecular machine-learning results can be interpreted on the public ClinTox CT_TOX benchmark. Audited 1,484 records and found 19 duplicated structures carrying contradictory labels; excluding them left 1,442 molecules with 93 positives. Compared logistic regression, random forest, and XGBoost against a GINE graph neural network across achiral and chirality-aware fingerprints under random versus scaffold-grouped validation. Curation, stereochemical encoding, and repeated training each shifted measured average precision, and repeated graph-network runs varied substantially across seeds and compute backends. The findings characterize this benchmark; they do not establish clinical safety prediction, calibrated probabilities, or independently validated generalization.

↳ Python · RDKit · XGBoost · Graph Neural Networks · Data Curation · Scaffold Validation · Reproducibility

Computational Hemodynamics and Physics-Guided Machine Learning for Arterial Stenosis Prediction

↳ Used SimVascular to model blood flow through idealized arteries with 0%, 30%, 50%, and 70% narrowing. Reading the Generator’s Handwriting — Tokenizer-Aware Fraud Detection on LLM-Synthesized Data

↳ For Kaggle’s Playground Series S6E11, predicted credit-card fraud across 699,960 synthetic transactions. Showed that the GPT-2 model that generated the data left fraud signal in decimal endings and number tokens, a pattern absent from the original NumPy-simulated data. Features built from those tokens raised a single LightGBM from 0.94674 to 0.95063 out-of-fold ROC-AUC, and a blend of shallow trees, a linear model and a neural network reached 0.95099 under nested cross-validation. The token features exploit how the data were generated, so they would not transfer to real transactions.

↳ Fraud Detection · LightGBM · Target Encoding · Nested Cross-Validation · Kaggle competition
